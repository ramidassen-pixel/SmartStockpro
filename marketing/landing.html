Questions

Before you start

Do I need a computer?

No. A phone with a browser is enough. You can also use a tablet or computer, and switch between them.

Does it work without internet?

The app opens and keeps working when the connection drops, then saves your records online when you are back.

Can I change the currency later?

Yes, in Settings. Amounts you already entered are not converted, so pick your main currency at the start.

Can my staff steal or delete records?

Staff accounts can sell, but cannot delete sales or see profit unless you allow it. You approve every new staff account.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan to keep going. If you do not, nothing is charged.

Can you set it up for me?

Yes. Send your product list and we will load it into the app for you on the Business plan.
