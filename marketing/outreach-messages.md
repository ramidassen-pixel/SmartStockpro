## Who to message first

1. Shops you already buy from: they know your face.
2. Shops with many customers buying on credit: the debt tracking is the strongest pitch.
3. Phone, electronics and pharmacy shops: lots of items, lots of stock to count.
4. Local business and market groups on Facebook and WhatsApp.

Aim for 30–50 personal messages today. Expect about 1 in 10 to say yes to a trial.
